### React Leadership Council Operations Policy

*A policy of the React Project*

*Adopted September 8, 2026  ·  Last amended September 8, 2026*

</div>

This policy establishes the operational procedures of the React Leadership Council. This policy may be amended by the Leadership Council through its public policy decision process.

### 1. Council Representatives

#### 1.1 Term

Each Council representative serves a term of one year unless replaced by their working group in accordance with the Working Group Policy. Terms may be renewed without limit, subject to the working group's internal selection process.

#### 1.2 Candidate Criteria

To be eligible to serve as a Council representative, an individual must:

1. be a member in good standing of the working group they will represent;
2. have demonstrated sustained participation in the Project;
3. agree to abide by the Project's Code of Conduct and the Charter; and
4. disclose any material affiliations or interests that could create a conflict of interest, in accordance with any Council-adopted conflict-of-interest disclosure procedure.

#### 1.3 Alternates

Each working group may designate one alternate representative to attend Council deliberations and act on the working group's behalf when the primary representative is unavailable. Alternates must satisfy the candidate criteria in Section 1.2 and must be designated to the Council in writing.

#### 1.4 Vacancies

1. A vacancy occurs when a Council representative resigns, is removed by their working group, leaves the working group, or otherwise becomes unable to serve.
2. Upon a vacancy, the affected working group must designate a replacement representative as promptly as practical, in accordance with the Working Group Policy and the candidate criteria in Section 1.2.
3. Where a working group is unable to designate a replacement representative within 30 days, the Council may temporarily exclude that working group from quorum calculations until a representative is designated.

#### 1.5 Participation Requirements

1. Council representatives are expected to actively participate in Council deliberations through the designated communication spaces.
2. A representative who has not participated in Council deliberations for two consecutive meetings, or 45 days of active deliberations may be considered non-participating by the Council. The Council may notify the working group of the non-participation and request that the working group designate a replacement or alternate.
3. Sustained non-participation by a Council representative may be grounds for the Council to formally request that the working group designate a replacement.

### 2. Council Operations

#### 2.1 Designated Communication Spaces

Council deliberations are conducted in the following designated communication spaces:

1. Public Github Repository — primary forum for public Council proposals, deliberations, and decisions;
2. Public Discord Channel — for synchronous coordination among Council representatives;
3. Private Mailing List or Private Discord Channel — for private deliberations as permitted under Section 3.d. of the Charter.

Proposals subject to public process under Section 3.c. of the Charter must be posted in the public Council forum.

#### 2.2 Quorum

Quorum for Council decisions is 50% of the total number of working groups. An alternate participating on behalf of a primary representative counts toward quorum on behalf of that working group.

#### 2.3 Minimum Feedback Period

A Council decision subject to public proposal under Section 3.c. of the Charter takes effect only after a minimum feedback period from the date the proposal was first posted in the designated public communication space. The minimum feedback period is:

1. 10 days for routine public policy decisions;
2. 14 days for decisions amending this policy, the Working Group Policy, or the React Change Proposal Policy; and
3. 30 days for proposed amendments to the Charter, as required by Section 11 of the Charter.

These are minimums. The Council may extend the feedback period on any given proposal.

#### 2.4 Recording of Decisions

1. The Council maintains a public record of decisions, including the proposal text, the participating representatives, the outcome, and the date the decision took effect.
2. Where a decision was made wholly or partly in private under Section 3.d. of the Charter, the public record indicates that a private decision was made and provides as much non-confidential context as is practical.

### 3. React Foundation Representative

#### 3.1 Selection

1. The Council designates one of its representatives to serve as the Project's technical representative to the React Foundation board.
2. The selection is made through the Council's public policy decision process.

#### 3.2 Term

The React Foundation representative serves a term of one year, renewable without limit through the Council's public policy decision process. The Council may replace the representative at any time through the same process.

#### 3.3 Responsibilities

The React Foundation representative:

1. communicates the Project's technical position to the React Foundation board on matters where the Foundation's organizational decisions intersect with the Project's technical direction;
2. reports to the Council on Foundation matters that may affect the Project; and
3. does not commit the Project to any position, agreement, or obligation without authorization from the Council.

#### 3.4 Vacancy

Upon a vacancy in the React Foundation representative role, the Council selects a replacement through the process described in Section 3.1.
